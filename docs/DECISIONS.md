# Approved Decisions

## Business decisions

1. FSquaro will operate as a brand website with a product catalogue, rather than a complete e-commerce checkout platform.
2. Purchase actions will send visitors to verified external marketplaces.
3. Product prices will not be shown on the website.
4. Search is part of the first release.
5. FSquaro will support multiple product categories. The existing jewellery and accessories catalogue is the current selection; food and other categories can be added once their product data and imagery are available.

## Technical decisions

1. The website uses Next.js with the App Router, React, strict TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS.
2. Lucide will be the icon library when interface icons are introduced.
3. Light and dark themes are required.
4. Initial product content will be local structured data.
5. The initial release will not include a database, backend, authentication, payment gateway, or shopping cart.

## Brand and design decisions

1. The visual direction is Modern Brand: clean, professional, spacious, and premium.
2. The approved core palette is primary navy `#061D35`, secondary navy `#082B4F`, accent blue `#007BFF`, light background `#FFFFFF`, secondary background `#F4F7FB`, and border `#E2E8F0`.
3. The default visual background is light; dark theme support remains required.
4. Official FSquaro logo assets must be used as provided; the logo must not be recreated or modified.

## Deferred decisions

1. Public product names, factual descriptions, specifications, and product-to-image mappings require owner review before a detailed product catalogue is published.
2. Verified marketplace URLs, contact details, and social destinations must be supplied before they are added to the website.
